About Anveshan

Learn about our mission, technology, and the team behind Anveshan

Our Mission

Anveshan, which means "discovery" or "search" in Sanskrit, was founded with a simple yet powerful mission: to leverage technology and community power to quickly locate missing children and Alzheimer's patients.

Every year, thousands of families experience the trauma of having a loved one go missing. Traditional search methods are often slow and fragmented. Anveshan addresses this critical gap by creating a unified digital platform that connects families, community members, and authorities in real-time.

Our Technology

At the heart of Anveshan is our advanced AI-powered recognition system:

  • MTCNN Detection: Accurate face detection even in challenging conditions
  • FaceNet Embeddings: Creates unique facial signatures for precise matching
  • Cosine Similarity Matching: Measures similarity between facial embeddings with high precision
  • Real-time Alert System: Instant notifications when potential matches are found
