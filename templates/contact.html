Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly does Anveshan respond to missing person reports?

We prioritize all missing person reports and typically initiate search efforts within 15-30 minutes of receiving a complete report. Our AI system immediately begins scanning community reports and alerts nearby volunteers.

Is my personal information secure?

Yes, we take data privacy very seriously. All personal information is encrypted and stored securely. We only share necessary information with authorized volunteers and law enforcement agencies involved in search efforts.

How accurate is the facial recognition technology?

Our AI system achieves 92% accuracy in matching faces under optimal conditions. All matches are verified by both our system administrators and, when possible, family members before any reunification actions are taken.

Can I volunteer to help with search efforts?

Absolutely! We welcome community volunteers. You can sign up through our website and will receive training on how to effectively participate in search efforts while maintaining safety protocols.
